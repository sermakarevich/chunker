# us_ai_deepfake_legislation

**Parent:** [[content/L1/us-ai-policy-and-investment|us-ai-policy-and-investment]] — US AI policy combines state and federal legislation targeting deepfakes and electoral manipulation, significant public funding for basic and applied AI research, and varied integration of computing in education.

The provided text details aspects of US policy and legislation regarding AI and deepfakes. In the United States, legislative efforts are focused on managing AI-generated content and deepfakes. Specifically, state-level regulations and federal proposals aim to address the creation and dissemination of deceptive media. These efforts often focus on transparency, requiring that AI-generated content be labeled, and protecting individuals' likenesses from unauthorized AI synthesis. Additionally, some regulations seek to penalize the use of deepfakes in elections to prevent voter manipulation and protect the integrity of democratic processes.
