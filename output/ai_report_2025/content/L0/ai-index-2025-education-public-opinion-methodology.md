# ai-index-2025-education-public-opinion-methodology

**Parent:** [[content/L1/ai-index-methodology-data-sources|ai-index-methodology-data-sources]] — The 2025 AI Index Report utilizes the US Federal Register, Legiscan API, and Ipsos surveys for policy and public opinion data, while tracking AI graduates via CIP codes 11.01, 11.02, and 11.07 and K-12 standards via a CSTA/IACE dataset of 10,000 standards.

The Artificial Intelligence Index Report 2025 details its data sources and methodologies for analyzing education and public opinion. To identify graduates in AI-related fields, the report used the Completions survey, which tracks students completing postsecondary education programs. Graduates were identified by their first major according to the Classification of Instructional Programs (CIP) codes, specifically including Computer and Information Sciences, General (11.01), Computer Programming (11.02), and Computer Science (11.07).

Regarding K-12 education, the AI Index's count of graduates in AI-related fields differs from previous years because the AI Index collaborated with the Computer Science Teachers Association (CSTA) and the Institute for Advancing Computing Education (IACE) to publish a State Standards Comparison report in December 2024. This report includes a dataset of approximately 10,000 state-adopted K-12 standards, available as a spreadsheet and a Python notebook. Notably, the standards for Colorado and Virginia are not included in this dataset as they were adopted in late 2024.

For broader educational data, the report utilized the OECD Data Explorer, specifically the table 'Number of enrolled students, graduates and new entrants by field of education.' The methodology for this data is detailed in the 'Education at a Glance 2024 Sources, Methodologies and Technical Notes.'

Finally, regarding the analysis of public opinion in Chapter 8, the 2025 AI Index utilized a survey conducted by Ipsos. For brevity, the report does not republish the full Ipsos survey methodology, directing readers to the survey itself for more detailed information.
