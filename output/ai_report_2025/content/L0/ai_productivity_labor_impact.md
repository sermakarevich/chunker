# ai_productivity_labor_impact

**Parent:** [[content/L1/ai-productivity-labor-medical-bio-science|ai-productivity-labor-medical-bio-science]] — AI tools significantly boost productivity for lower-skilled workers and require organizational restructuring for maximum gain, while in science, AlphaFold and de novo protein design are transforming drug discovery and multimodal AI is integrating imaging, genomic, and clinical data for personalized medicine.

The report analyzes the impact of AI and automation on productivity and labor. In terms of productivity, a study of employees reveals that the use of AI tools has a significant effect on productivity, particularly for lower-skilled workers. Regarding labor, the report notes that while AI can automate certain tasks, it also creates new roles and requires a shift in skills. Specifically, in the context of labor markets, there is a growing demand for 'AI-complementary' skills. The report also highlights the importance of organizational adaptation to maximize the benefits of AI. For example, companies that restructure their workflows to integrate AI tend to see higher productivity gains than those that simply add AI tools to existing processes.
