# ai-index-report-2025-contributors-and-toc

**Parent:** [[content/L1/ai-index-report-2025-comprehensive-analysis|ai-index-report-2025-comprehensive-analysis]] — The Artificial Intelligence Index Report 2025 by Stanford HAI details a record $252.3 billion in global private AI investment and a 280-fold decrease in GPT-3.5 level inference costs, while noting that the top two frontier AI models now differ by only 0.7% in performance.

The Global AI Vibrancy tool will be updated in the summer of 2025. 

The AI Index is an independent initiative at the Stanford Institute for Human-Centered Artificial Intelligence (HAI) and was conceived within the One Hundred Year Study on Artificial Intelligence (AI100). The AI Index welcomes feedback and new ideas for the next year through contact with Nestor Maslej at nmaslej@stanford.edu. While the AI Index Report 2025 was authored by a team of human researchers, the writing process was aided by AI tools; specifically, the authors used ChatGPT and Claude to tighten and copy edit initial drafts. In this workflow, authors wrote the original copy and utilized AI tools during the editing process.
