Based on the provided documents, the analysis of global AI legislation and regulation shows a significant increase in legislative activity. In the United States, there is a marked contrast between federal and state-level activity; while federal legislative progress is slower, state-level activity is high. Between 2014 and 2024, a total of 133 bills related to AI were introduced in the U.S. Congress. In the U.S. states, there is a high volume of AI legislation, with 35 states introducing AI-related bills in 2023 alone. Globally, the EU AI Act represents a major regulatory effort, categorizing AI systems by risk level (unacceptable, high, limited, and minimal) and imposing strict obligations on high-risk systems. In China, regulation has focused on specific applications, such as deep synthesis and recommendation algorithms, requiring providers to ensure content is truthful and that users are notified when interacting with AI.
