# linkedin_ai_skills_methodology

**Parent:** [[content/L1/ai-workforce-methodology-and-metrics|ai-workforce-methodology-and-metrics]] — This block details the methodology for analyzing AI labor market trends using LinkedIn data and the 'Worldized' dataset, distinguishing between 'AI skills' and the broader 'AI-related skills' taxonomy to track skill penetration, AI-powered roles, and the transition of software engineers and data scientists into AI-centric work.

This document provides the methodology and definitions used for analyzing AI-related workforce and skill data, specifically focusing on metrics from LinkedIn. It defines two primary categories of skills: 'AI skills' (which include the use of AI tools and the development of AI) and 'AI-related skills' (which include the use of AI tools and the development of AI, but also include broader technical skills). To determine these metrics, the analysis uses the 'AI skills' and 'AI-related skills' taxonomies. The document outlines how 'AI skills' are measured by counting the number of users who list a specific AI skill on their profile, and how the 'AI-related skills' metric is calculated by identifying users with any skill from the broader taxonomy. It also describes the process of identifying 'AI-powered' roles by analyzing the frequency of AI skills within specific job titles. Additionally, the text explains how the 'AI skill' penetration is calculated as a percentage of the total workforce within a given region or industry.
