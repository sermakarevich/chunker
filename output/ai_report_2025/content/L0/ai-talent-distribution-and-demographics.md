# ai-talent-distribution-and-demographics

**Parent:** [[content/L1/ai-economic-labor-trends-2025|ai-economic-labor-trends-2025]] — The 2025 AI Index Report shows that the US leads in AI skill penetration, while growth is fastest in emerging markets, with demand expanding beyond technical roles into healthcare and finance.

Based on the 2025 AI report data, the distribution of AI talent and skills varies significantly by region and demographics. Regarding talent penetration, the United States leads with the highest relative penetration of AI skills. In terms of demographics, the report highlights a gender gap in AI talent; for example, in the US, the penetration of AI skills is notably higher among men than women. Regarding talent distribution across roles, the report shows a high concentration of AI skills in technical roles (such as software engineering and data science) compared to non-technical roles. Furthermore, the report notes that the growth of AI talent has been most rapid in emerging markets, though the absolute number of skilled professionals remains highest in North America and Asia. The report also emphasizes the role of educational background, noting that individuals with advanced degrees in STEM fields are disproportionately represented in the AI talent pool.
