# ai-2024-model-releases-performance

**Parent:** [[content/L1/ai-technical-performance-2024-2025|ai-technical-performance-2024-2025]] — The Technical Performance chapter of the AI Index Report 2025 details how AI models in 2024 achieved unprecedented benchmark gains (e.g., SWE-bench rising from 4.4% to 71.7%) and closed the gap between open-weight and closed-weight models, as well as US and Chinese models (e.g., MMLU gap narrowing from 17.5 to 0.3 percentage points). It also highlights the lauch of advanced models like OpenAI's o1 and o3, and the lauch of Meta's Llama 3.1 405B.

The Technical Performance chapter of the Artificial Intelligence Index Report 2025, published by the AI Index Steering Committee at Stanford University's Institute for Human-Centered AI, provides a comprehensive overview of AI technical performance and significant model releases in 2024. 

### Significant Model and Dataset Releases of 2024
As selected by the AI Index Steering Committee, the following were some of the most notable AI releases in 2024:

*   **January 19, 2024 - Stable LM 2 (LLM, Stability AI):** An enhanced version of the original Stable LM with 1.6 billion parameters, designed for efficient operation on portable devices like smartphones and laptops.
*   **February 8, 2024 - Aya Dataset (Dataset, Cohere for AI, Beijing Academy of AI, Cohere, Binghamton University):** A collection of 513 million prompt-completion pairs across 114 languages, representing a milestone in multilingual instruction tuning.
*   **February 15, 2024 - Gemini 1.5 Pro (LLM, Google DeepMind):** This model established a new benchmark with a 1 million token context window, significantly exceeding the 128K token limit of GPT-4 Turbo.
*   **February 20, 2024 - SDXL-Lightning (Text-to-image, ByteDance):** A fast text-to-image system that generates high-quality synthetic images in under a second using progressive adversarial distillation rather than diffusion-based techniques.
*   **March 4, 2024 - Claude 3 (LLM, Anthropic):** An LLM that outperforms GPT-4 and Gemini on nearly all industry benchmarks while reducing incorrect prompt refusals and increasing accuracy.
*   **March 7, 2024 - Inflection-2.5 (LLM, Inflection AI):** A model with GPT-4-level performance that uses only 40% of the computing resources of GPT-4. Microsoft acquired Inflection for $650 million two weeks after the model's release.
*   **March 19, 2024 - Moirai and LOTSA (Model/Dataset, Salesforce):** Moirai is a foundation model for universal forecasting, and LOTSA is a large-scale time series dataset containing 27 billion observations across nine domains.
*   **March 27, 2024 - DBRX (LLM, Databricks):** An open-source mixture-of-experts (MoE) transformer decoder-only model with 132B parameters (36B active per input), trained on 12 trillion tokens, surpassing similar small MoE models like Mixtral and Grok.
*   **April 2, 2024 - Stable Audio 2 (Text-to-song and song-to-song, Stability AI):** A song generator that supports audio-to-audio functionality, allowing users to manipulate songs via natural language prompts.
*   **April 17, 2024 - Llama 3 (LLM, Meta):** A series of text-based models with 8B and 70B parameters, ranking among the highest performing models of their size.
*   **May 13, 2024 - GPT-4o (Multimodal, OpenAI):** A multimodal model that processes and generates text, audio, images, and video; it can respond to audio in as little as 320 milliseconds.
*   **June 7, 2024 - Qwen2 (LLM, Alibaba):** A series of base and instruction-tuned models that rival Llama 3-70B and Mixtral-8x22B in performance.
*   **June 17, 2024 - Runway Gen-3 (Text-to-video and image-to-video, Runway):** A video generation model that excels in creating photorealistic humans with vivid emotionality.
*   **July 23, 2024 - Llama 3.1 405B (LLM, Meta):** Meta's largest model with 405B parameters, which became the most capable openly available foundation model, rivaling many closed models.
*   **August 12, 2024 - Falcon Mamba (LLM, Technology Innovation Institute in Abu Dhabi):** A 7B parameter model based on the Mamba State Space Language Model (SSLM) architecture, which is more efficient than transformer-based models by dynamically adjusting parameters.
*   **August 13, 2024 - Grok-2 (Text-to-text and text-to-image, xAI):** An advanced reasoning and problem-solving model that rivaled leading models shortly after xAI was founded in March 2023.
*   **August 15, 2024 - Imagen 3 (Text-to-image, Google Labs):** An image generator that achieved the highest Elo score on the GenAI-Bench image benchmark.
*   **August 22, 2024 - Jamba 1.5 (LLM, AI21 Labs):** The first LLM to combine state-space models with transformers to enhance speed while preserving quality.
*   **August 29, 2024 - SynthID v2 (Tool, Google):** A watermarking and identification software supporting AI-generated images, video, audio, and text.
*   **September 11, 2024 - NotebookLM Podcast Tool (Text-to-podcast, Google Labs):** An end-to-end AI podcast generator used for study and summary purposes.
*   **September 12, 2024 - o1-preview (Language, math, biology, OpenAI):** The first in the "o series" designed for advanced reasoning, proving significantly more powerful than GPT in math, science, and coding.
*   **September 17, 2024 - NVLM (D, H, X) (Vision, language, Nvidia):** Three open-access models for vision-language tasks that achieved top scores on VQAv2 and OCRBench.
*   **September 19, 2024 - Qwen2.5 (LLM, Alibaba):** A series of foundation models including efficient smaller models and specialized coding and math models.
*   **October 16, 2024 - Ministral (LLM, Mistral):** A pair of compact models (3B and 8B parameters) that outperformed Gemma and Llama models of similar size.
*   **October 22, 2024 - Anthropic Computer Use (Agentic Capability, Anthropic):** A feature for Claude 3.5 Sonnet allowing the model to autonomously move the cursor and type on a user's computer in real time.
*   **October 28, 2024 - Apple Intelligence (iPhone feature, Apple):** A suite of AI features including Image Playground, Genmoji, and Siri integration with ChatGPT.
*   **December 3, 2024 - Nova Pro (Multimodal, Amazon):** The most powerful model in AWS's Nova family, specializing in the analysis of financial documents.
*   **December 11, 2024 - Gemini 2 (LLM, Google DeepMind):** An improved version of Gemini that is twice as fast as Gemini 1.5 Pro and includes computer control and image/audio generation.
*   **December 12, 2024 - Sora (Text-to-video, OpenAI):** A video generation model capable of creating 1080p videos up to 20 seconds long (for ChatGPT Pro users) and 720p videos up to five seconds long (for ChatGPT Plus users).
*   **December 13, 2024 - Global MMLU (Dataset, Cohere):** A multilingual evaluation set with professionally translated MMLU questions across 42 languages to reduce Western bias.
*   **December 20, 2024 - o3 (beta) (Multimodal, OpenAI):** A frontier model that outperforms previous models in PhD-level science, research math, competition math, and SWE, achieving a record 87.5% on the ARC-AGI private holdout set.
*   **December 27, 2024 - DeepSeek-V3 (LLM, DeepSeek):** An open-source model developed with fewer computing resources that outperforms leading models on GPQA and MMLU benchmarks.

### State of AI Performance
AI systems have continued to improve in 2024, exceeding human performance on several previously challenging benchmarks. 

In areas where human ability still surpasses AI, the gap is shrinking rapidly. For instance, on the MATH benchmark for competition-level mathematics, state-of-the-art AI systems are now 7.9 percentage points ahead of human performance, compared to a 0.3-point gap in 2024. On the MMMU benchmark for complex, multidisciplinary expert-level questions, the best 2024 model, o1, scored 78.2%, which is only 4.4 percentage points below the human benchmark of 82.6%.

Additionally, there is a narrowing gap between closed-weight and open-weight LLMs. According to the Chatbot Arena Leaderboard, the leading closed-weight model outperformed the top open-weight model by 8.0% in early January 2024; by February 2025, this gap had narrowed to 1.7%.
