# ai_legislation_education_methodology

**Parent:** [[content/L1/ai-index-methodology-data-sources|ai-index-methodology-data-sources]] — The 2025 AI Index Report utilizes the US Federal Register, Legiscan API, and Ipsos surveys for policy and public opinion data, while tracking AI graduates via CIP codes 11.01, 11.02, and 11.07 and K-12 standards via a CSTA/IACE dataset of 10,000 standards.

The provided text details the methodology and data sources used to analyze legislation, policy, and education regarding AI. To track legislation and policy, the researchers analyzed the US Federal Register and used the 'Legiscan' API to monitor state-level legislative activity. For education, the analysis focused on the integration of AI into curricula, specifically examining the 'Common Sense Education' AI curriculum and various university-level AI course offerings. The researchers also reviewed governmental reports from the US Department of Education and the OECD to understand the policy landscape surrounding AI in education. Regarding the technicalities of the legislation analysis, the researchers employed Natural Language Processing (NLP) techniques, including keyword extraction and topic modeling (LDA), to categorize bills based on themes such as 'AI ethics,' 'automation,' and 'algorithmic transparency.'
