# ai_political_influence_risks

**Parent:** [[content/L1/ai-technical-performance-2025-3|ai-technical-performance-2025-3]] — The AI Index Report 2025 analyzes technical milestones, such as OpenAI's o3 achieving 87.7% on GPQA Diamond and 87.5% on ARC-AGI, alongside the robotics expansion of Figure 02 and Waymo's 150,000 weekly rides, while addressing systemic bias and the use of AI-generated deepfakes in the 2024 election cycle.

The 2024/2025 report on AI ethics and governance highlights several ways AI is being used to influence political and social discourse, specifically regarding the 2024 election cycle. A primary concern is the use of AI to create deceptive content. Examples include the use of AI to generate fake images or audio to mislead voters, and the use of AI-generated 'deepfakes' to impersonate political figures. Additionally, AI can be used to automate the creation of disinformation campaigns, allowing for the rapid spread of false narratives at scale. The report notes that while AI can be a tool for efficiency, its application in political contexts often risks undermining democratic processes by eroding trust in information and manipulating public opinion.
