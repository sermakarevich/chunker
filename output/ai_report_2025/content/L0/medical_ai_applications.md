# medical_ai_applications

**Parent:** [[content/L1/ai-healthcare-medical-diagnostics|ai-healthcare-medical-diagnostics]] — AI in healthcare is utilizing pre-training on general images to overcome medical data scarcity, while employing grounding mechanisms to prevent LLM hallucinations in clinical settings. Applications range from AI-powered surgery and wearable monitoring to the use of synthetic data for privacy-enhanced medical research.

In the context of healthcare and medicine, the application of AI is evolving through various specialized uses. In clinical settings, AI is used to identify and manage patients, and it is increasingly applied to the identification of patients based on specific health criteria. Regarding the use of synthetic data, synthetic data is being utilized to enhance data privacy and utility in medical research. In terms of medical imaging, AI is applied to improve the accuracy of diagnostics. For the management of medical records, AI is used to automate the extraction of information from unstructured clinical notes to better populate electronic health records. Furthermore, in drug discovery, AI is used to predict the properties of potential drug candidates and optimize terms of molecular design. In the realm of surgery, AI-powered robotics are used to increase precision and reduce the invasiveness of procedures. Finally, for patient monitoring, AI is used to analyze real-time data from wearables to detect anomalies and alert providers to potential health crises.
