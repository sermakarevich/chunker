The synthesis deeply integrates technical data processing and classification methodologies critical to modern pipeline engineering. Procurement analysis for AI followed a multistep workflow where a 2023 study by the U.S. Government Accountability Office (GAO) revealed that 49 agencies, including 25 executive branch agencies, failed to report data to USAspending, representing over $5 billion in net outlays for fiscal year 2022. To isolate AI-related procurement, regular expression (regex) matching was applied to contract and grant awards, categorizing documents as either “non AI-related” or “AI-related.” Validation utilized a BERTopic model and a pretrained DeBERTA transformer to assess probability scores. Awards with relevance scores below 20% underwent manual review, while higher scores were confirmed, supplemented by manual review of all high-value tenders. Processing data from the TED (Tenders Electronic Daily) database required handling inconsistent XML tag names, aggregated versus lot-specific descriptions, and linguistic diversity via translation using the deeptranslator tool and the Google Translator engine. Subsequent NLP preprocessing involved removing stop words and special characters, POS tagging, converting to lowercase, lemmatization, and replacing numerical measures with a <NUM> tag. This multi-stage validation and normalization pipeline ensures signal integrity comparable to rigorous data quality checks in ETL orchestration.
