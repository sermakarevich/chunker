## Top-Level Summaries
- [[content/L2/ai-index-report-2025-comprehensive-analysis-2|ai-index-report-2025-comprehensive-analysis-2]] — The AI Index Report 2025 details a record $252.3 billion in global private AI investment in 2024, a 280-fold reduction in GPT-3.5 level inference costs, and the convergence of frontier model performance where the top two models differ by only 0.7%.
- [[content/L2/ai-index-2025-technical-ecosystem-synthesis|ai-index-2025-technical-ecosystem-synthesis]] — The AI Index Report 2025 highlights a shift in AI research and patent leadership toward East Asia and the Pacific, alongside a convergence in performance between US and Chinese models, and a significant decrease in inference costs for GPT-3.5 equivalent models from $20 to $0.07 per million2024.
- [[content/L2/ai-technical-performance-2025|ai-technical-performance-2025]] — The 2025 AI Index Report analyzes the convergence of frontier models, noting that o3 achieved an 87.5% score on ARC-AGI and o1 solved 74.4% of IMO qualifying exams, while the gap between US and Chinese models narrowed to 1.7% on the LMSYS Chatbot Arena.
- [[content/L2/ai-technical-performance-2025-2|ai-technical-performance-2025-2]] — The AI Index Report 2025 Technical Performance chapter details record-breaking AI reasoning (OpenAI o3's 87.7% on GPQA Diamond) and robotics (Waymo's 150,000 weekly paid rides), while analyzing the AI Incident Database's record 233 incidents in 2024.
- [[content/L2/ai-index-2025-technical-performance-economics|ai-index-2025-technical-performance-economics]] — The 2025 AI Index Report details record-breaking AI performance, such as OpenAI o3 achieving 87.7% on GPQA Diamond and 87.5% on ARC-AGI, alongside the deployment of Waymo's 150,000 weekly paid robotaxi rides and the expansion of AI's strategic economic integration.
- [[content/L2/ai-impact-labor-science-healthcare|ai-impact-labor-science-healthcare]] — AI is increasing productivity most significantly for lower-skilled workers and shifting labor demand toward AI-complementary skills, while in science, tools like AlphaFold and Gluon are enabling de novo protein design and drug discovery. In healthcare, AI is improving diagnostics via X-ray/MRI/CT analysis and multimodal integration of genomic and genomic data, though clinical deployment is hindered by LLM hallucinations, Pydantic v2 'NoneType' attribute errors in system logs, and the need for rigorous clinical validation.
- [[content/L2/global-ai-policy-and-scientific-application|global-ai-policy-and-scientific-application]] — Between 2014 and 2024, the US Congress introduced 133 AI-related bills, while 35 states did so in 2023, complementing the EU's risk-based AI Act (categorizing systems into unacceptable, high, limited, and minimal risk) and China's specific regulations on recommendation algorithms and deep synthesis.
- [[content/L2/us-ai-education-policy-perception|us-ai-education-policy-perception]] — The synthesis covers the systemic disparities in US K-12 AI education and the rise of micro-credentials to meet workforce demand, while highlighting the policy divide among US policymakers regarding economic growth versus security and job displacement risks.
- [[content/L1/ai-performance-evaluation-methodology|ai-performance-evaluation-methodology]] — The 2025 AI Index Report utilizes benchmark datasets, metrics like accuracy, F1 score, and perplexity, and human-in-the-loop evaluation to measure LLM performance while avoiding data contamination. It also employs a keyword-based analysis of literature focusing on data privacy and security to assess Responsible AI frameworks.
- [[content/L1/ai-workforce-methodology-and-metrics|ai-workforce-methodology-and-metrics]] — This block details the methodology for analyzing AI labor market trends using LinkedIn data and the 'Worldized' dataset, distinguishing between 'AI skills' and the broader 'AI-related skills' taxonomy to track skill penetration, AI-powered roles, and the transition of software engineers and data scientists into AI-centric work.
- [[content/L1/medical-scientific-ai-methodology|medical-scientific-ai-methodology]] — This block details the evaluation of medical and scientific AI using Med-AI and Sci-AI benchmarks, including the USMLE for medical reasoning, while highlighting technical advancements in protein folding, pathology, and the sHIF framework.
- [[content/L1/ai-index-methodology-data-sources|ai-index-methodology-data-sources]] — The 2025 AI Index Report utilizes the US Federal Register, Legiscan API, and Ipsos surveys for policy and public opinion data, while tracking AI graduates via CIP codes 11.01, 11.02, and 11.07 and K-12 standards via a CSTA/IACE dataset of 10,000 standards.
