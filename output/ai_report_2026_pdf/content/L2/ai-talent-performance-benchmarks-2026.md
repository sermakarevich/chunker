# ai-talent-performance-benchmarks-2026

**Parent:** [[index]]

The 2026 Artificial Intelligence Index Report provides a detailed analysis of global AI talent, the technical performance of frontier models, and the evaluative frameworks used to measure progress. The report spans data from 2010 to 2026, focusing on technical outputs and the distribution of human capital. 

### AI Talent Distribution, Education, and Mobility

#### Geographic and Per Capita Distribution
In 2025, the United States led the world in absolute numbers of top AI authors and inventors with 220,520 individuals. India followed with 50,460, and Germany with 48,520. Other significant contributors included the United Kingdom (34,370), Canada (31,450), and France (18,820). A secondary tier of talent included Australia (14,540), the Netherlands (13,960), Italy (13,230), Brazil (11,100), and Switzerland (9,980), followed by Spain (9,170), Sweden (8,520), Singapore (6,610), Japan (6,280), South Korea (5,960), Israel (5,190), Finland (4,380), Denmark (3,900), Saudi Arabia (3,430), and the United Arab Emirates (3,090).

When measured per 100,000 inhabitants in 2025, Switzerland ranked first with 110.45 AI authors and inventors, followed by Singapore (109.51). Other high-ranking nations included Sweden (80.63), Finland (77.61), the Netherlands (77.61), Canada (76.16), and Denmark (65.25). The United States ranked eighth with 64.84, followed by Germany (58.10), Australia (53.43), Israel (52.01), and the United Kingdom (49.64). The United Arab Emirates (28.38), France (27.46), and Italy (22.43) recorded lower per capita rankings.

### Technical Performance of Frontier AI Models

#### Model Benchmarks and Human Baselines
By 2025, frontier systems reached or exceeded human levels (100% baseline) on ImageNet (image classification), SuperGLUE (English language understanding), MMLU (multitask language understanding), MMMU (multimodal reasoning), GPQA Diamond (PhD-level science), and AIME (mathematical reasoning). However, gaps persist in autonomous software engineering (SWE-bench Verified) and agent-based multimodal computer use (OSWorld). While these are below human baseline, improvement is accelerating; SWE-bench Verified performance rose from ~60% in 2024 to nearly 100% in 2025.

#### Capability Convergence and Competition
As of March 2026, leading models have converged in capability. The Arena Elo ratings show the top tier is dominated by six companies: Anthropic (1,503), xAI (1,495), Google (1,494), OpenAI (1,481), Alibaba (1,449), and DeepSeek (1,424). The top four are separated by fewer than 25 Elo points. Other notable scores include Mistral AI (1,416) and Meta (1,335), with Meta's performance flattening since early 2025. 

Competition has shifted from raw capability to cost, reliability, and real-world utility. The gap between closed-weight and open-weight models fluctuates. In May 2023, GPT-4-0314 (closed) led Vicuna-13B (open) by 174 points (15.2%). By August 2024, models like Llama-3.1-405B, Mixtral, and WizardLM narrowed this gap to 7 points (0.5%). As of March 2026, Claude Opus 4.6 (closed, 1,503) leads GLM-5 (open, 1,454) by 49 points (3.4%).

#### U.S. vs. China Technical Performance
The technical gap between the U.S. and China has effectively closed. In February 2025, DeepSeek-R1 (Chinese) briefly matched the top U.S. model. In March 2026, the top U.S. model (Claude Opus 4.6, 1,503) led the top Chinese model (Dola-Seed-2.0 Preview, 1,464) by 39 points (2.7%). Over the past year, this gap has fluctuated between near parity and low single digits.

#### Language Capabilities and MMLU-Pro
To prevent memorization and reduce prompt sensitivity, the MMLU-Pro benchmark (introduced 2024) uses 12,000 questions in a 10-option multiple-choice format. Accuracy on MMLU-Pro is generally 16%–33% lower than on the original MMLU. As of early 2026, the top 15 models are clustered above 87%, with only a 4 percentage point spread. Gemini-3.1-Pro leads with 91.16%, followed by Gemini-3-Pro (11/25) at 90.10% and GPT-o1 at 89.30%.

#### Benchmarking Limitations and "Jagged Intelligence"
Benchmarking is hindered by benchmark saturation, opacity in reporting, and contamination. A notable example was the criticism of Meta's Llama 4 in 2025 for being optimized specifically for benchmarks. 

Technical audits revealed high error rates in invalid question detection (precision@50). For MMLU Math and OpenBookQA, the error rate is 2%; for MMLU Cli and MMLU Med, it is 6%; for AIR-Bench, it is 9%; for MedQA, it is 23%; for ThaiExam, it is 26%; and for GSM8K, it is 42%. 

To combat these, new frameworks have been proposed: a statistical analysis framework by Truong et al. (2025) with 84% precision in flagging problematic items, and a 'certificate-grade' community-governed framework by Cheng et al. (2025).

Models exhibit "jagged intelligence," where they excel at complex tasks but fail at simple ones. For example, Gemini Deep Think won a gold medal at the 2025 IMO (35 points), but on ClockBench, top AI models read analog clocks correctly only 50.6% of the time (humans are 90.1%).

#### Domain-Specific and Agent Performance
- **Coding**: Models struggle with multi-step reasoning and long-horizon tasks. The 'CodeContests' benchmark is used to evaluate efficiency and correctness against hidden test cases.
- **Mathematics**: A gap remains between simple arithmetic (System 1) and complex problem-solving (System 2). MATH and GSM8K are used for assessments.
- **Professional Domains**: Performance in corporate finance, mortgage processing, tax, and legal reasoning is between 60% and 90%, with top models separated by as little as 3 percentage points. 
- **AI Agents**: Accuracy on OSWorld rose from 12% to 66.3%, currently within 6 percentage points of human performance.
- **Robotics**: A massive 'sim-to-real' gap exists. RLBench shows high simulation success rates (89.4%), but real-world household task success is only 12%.
- **Autonomous Vehicles**: In 2025, Waymo conducted ~450,000 weekly trips, and China's Apollo Go completed 11 million fully driverless rides (a 175% YoY increase).

#### Timeline of Major Releases (2025-2026)
- **DeepSeek-R1 (Jan 20, 2025)**: Uses GRPO for reinforcement learning without labeled data. Its release triggered a trillion-dollar market value decline in U.S. tech stocks.
- **Gemini 2.5 Pro (Mar 25, 2025)**: Multimodal model with 1M tokens context, scored ~63.8% on SWE-Bench Verified.
- ** uma 2025 (Sep 29, 2025)**: Claude Sonnet 4.5 achieved 61.4% on OSWorld and 77.2%+ on SWE-bench Verified.
- **GPT-5.1 (Nov 12, 2025)**: A faster multimodal model scoring ~76.3% on SWE-bench Verified (vs. ~72.8%).

#### Software Layer Failures
The report identifies software layer errors, such as the 'NoneType' object has no attribute 'model_dump' error, as indicators of code execution errors in the software layer interacting with the LLM.

### Summary of Evaluation Paradigms
To move beyond synthetic benchmarks, researchers are proposing 'Centaur evaluations' to measure human-AI collaboration and interpretability, and addressing the 'sim-to-real' gap in robotics via domain randomization and the scaling of training data.
